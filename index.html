4-week plan

Lean, tight & less puffy

Gym plan, Boojum-friendly food plan and a skincare routine. The goal: lose fat, tighten the belly, sides, chest and arms, and de-puff the face. Leaner, not bulky.

What 4 weeks can really do

Realistic in 4 weeks
  • Face noticeably less puffy, often within 7–14 days (salt, alcohol, sleep and water matter most)
  • About 2–4 kg down on the scale
  • Waist 2–5 cm smaller
  • Stronger, tighter-looking chest, arms and shoulders
Takes longer
  • Fully flat belly and visible abs: 3–6 months of the same habits
  • You can't burn fat from one spot. Belly, sides and chest fat go as your overall body fat drops, and the training shapes what's underneath.
The formula: a moderate calorie deficit, high protein, 4 lifting sessions a week, daily steps, and less salt and alcohol. Weeks 1–4 build the habit. Then you keep going.

The rules

Protein every meal

30–50 g per meal. It keeps you full and keeps muscle while fat comes off.

No liquid calories

Water, black coffee, tea, zero-sugar drinks. Cut fizzy drinks, juice and alcohol for the 4 weeks. Alcohol is a big cause of face puffiness.

Watch the salt

Mexican food is salty, and salt holds water in the face. Drink more water and eat potassium foods (banana, potatoes, beans, spinach).

One treat meal a week

Full burrito, a takeaway, whatever you like. Enjoy it, then straight back to the plan.

De-puff the face

Most facial puffiness is water and overall body fat, not skin. Fix these first, and the skincare does the rest.

Salt down

The biggest one with Mexican food. Go easy on extra cheese and salty sauces, and don't add salt at home.

Water up

2.5–3 L a day. More water means your body holds less of it.

No alcohol

4 weeks off. You'll see the difference in the face in about a week.

Sleep 7–9 h

Sleep on your back with a slightly higher pillow so fluid doesn't pool in the face overnight.

Cold in the morning

Splash with cold water or use an ice roller for 1–2 min. It quickly tightens up a puffy morning face.

Lose the body fat

The gym and food plans slim the face down too: cheeks and jawline get sharper as body fat drops.

Shave with the grain, use a fresh blade and shaving gel, and finish with moisturiser. If you get bumps, the azelaic acid helps.

Optional extras

An ice roller or gua sha for the morning (a cold spoon also works). A lip balm. A clean pillowcase twice a week.

If your face stays swollen even after cutting salt and alcohol, or your ankles or eyes swell too, see a GP to rule out allergies, thyroid or kidney issues.
