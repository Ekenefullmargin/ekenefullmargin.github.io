4-week plan

Lean, tight & less puffy

Gym plan, a food plan built around Mexican, kebabs and your usual snacks, and a routine to lose the face fat and puffiness. The goal: tighten the belly, sides, chest and arms. Leaner, not bulky.

What 4 weeks can really do

Realistic in 4 weeks
  • Face noticeably less puffy, often within 7–14 days (salt, alcohol, sleep and water matter most)
  • About 2–4 kg down on the scale
  • Waist 2–5 cm smaller
  • Stronger, tighter-looking chest, arms and shoulders
Takes longer
  • Fully flat belly and visible abs: 3–6 months of the same habits
  • You can't burn fat from one spot. Belly, sides and chest fat go as your overall body fat drops, and the training shapes what's underneath.
The formula: a moderate calorie deficit, high protein, 4 lifting sessions a week, daily steps, and less salt and alcohol. Weeks 1–4 build the habit. Then you keep going.

How your day of eating works

You don't need to change what you eat, only how you order it and how much. Every day is built from the same pieces:

Meal 1

Mexican

Lean bowl or tacos. ~550–700 kcal.

Meal 2

Kebab or Mexican

Chicken shish or a second bowl. ~550–750 kcal.

Protein top-up

Shake or yoghurt

Whey shake, skyr or Greek yoghurt. ~150–250 kcal, 25–35 g protein.

Snack budget

Biscuits or crêpe

Up to ~250 kcal a day. See the cheat sheet below.

That adds up to roughly 1,700–2,000 kcal. If your target on the Start tab is higher, add a banana, toast, or a bigger portion of rice to reach it. Don't go much lower than the target: being too hungry is how plans fall apart.

  • Burrito bowl, not a burrito (the big flour tortilla alone is ~300 kcal)

Ordering a kebab

Best order · ~550–750 kcal · 45–60 g protein

Chicken shish (grilled chicken pieces)

  • Chicken shish or chicken tikka, not doner. Doner meat is mostly fat.
  • In a pitta, or on a plate with salad (+ a small portion of rice if you're hungry)
  • Loads of salad: lettuce, onion, tomato, cabbage, cucumber
  • Chilli sauce instead of garlic mayo (garlic mayo is ~100 kcal per spoonful)
  • No chips, or a small portion shared on your treat day
  • Water or a zero-sugar drink, not a can of regular Coke (~140 kcal)

Easy wins

No liquid calories

Water, black coffee, tea, zero-sugar drinks. Cut regular fizzy drinks, juice and alcohol for the 4 weeks.

Protein every meal

Double meat at Mexican, shish at the kebab shop, a shake on top. It keeps you full and keeps muscle while fat comes off.

Eat slowly

Put the fork or kebab down between bites. It takes about 20 minutes for your body to register you're full.

Losing face fat: the honest version

There's no exercise that burns fat only from the face. Face fat comes off the same way as belly fat: through the calorie deficit, the gym plan and the steps. The good news is the face is often one of the first places people notice it. What makes a face look puffy day to day is mostly water, and you can fix that within 1–2 weeks with the habits below.

Daily targets

Water

3 L a day

See your exact number on the Start tab. Add 500–750 ml on gym days, and a big glass after every kebab or Mexican meal. Spread it out: 500 ml when you wake up, a bottle on shift, a glass with each meal. Pee should be pale yellow.

Sleep

7–9 hours

Under 6 hours makes you hungrier, hold more water and look puffier the next day. Same bedtime every night, even after a late shift. No phone 30 min before. Sleep on your back with a slightly higher pillow.

Salt

Under 6 g a day

One kebab or burrito can have 3–5 g on its own, so don't add any extra salt to food. Go easy on extra cheese and sauces. Eat potassium foods (bananas, potatoes, beans, spinach), which help flush salt.

Alcohol

Zero for 4 weeks

Alcohol is one of the biggest causes of a puffy face. Most people see the difference in about a week.

Face routine · every morning, 5 minutes

This de-puffs (water) and improves how the jaw and neck look. It works together with the fat loss.

1
Cold water or ice roller · 1 min
Splash your face with cold water 10 times, or roll an ice roller (or a cold spoon) over the cheeks, under the eyes and along the jaw.
2
Drainage massage · 2 min
With a little moisturiser, use your fingertips with light pressure. Stroke from the middle of your forehead out to the temples (5×), from beside the nose across the cheeks to the ears (5×), and from the chin along the jawline to below the ears (5×). Then from below the ears down the sides of the neck to the collarbone (5×). It pushes trapped fluid out of the face.
3
Chin tucks · 2 × 10
Stand tall, pull your chin straight back (give yourself a double chin) and hold 3 seconds. This fixes the forward-head posture that makes a double chin look bigger.
4
Neck stretch & jaw lift · 2 × 10
Tilt your head back to look at the ceiling, push your lower jaw forward, and hold 5 seconds. You'll feel it under the chin. This tones the neck muscles under the jaw.

These tone muscle and improve posture. They don't burn face fat. Skip "mewing" or jaw-chewing gadgets: there's no good evidence they slim the face, and chewing gadgets can hurt your jaw joint.

What slims the face the most

  1. The calorie deficit: Mexican and kebab ordered the lean way, biscuits within budget
  2. Steps + cardio: 8–10k steps a day, the Wednesday intervals and the gym finishers
  3. Water, sleep, salt and alcohol: the targets above
  4. The 5-minute morning routine
